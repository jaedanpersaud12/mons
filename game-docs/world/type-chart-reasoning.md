# Type chart: the reasoning

Status: Draft. Written alongside the Bible Quest "Write the type chart" quest on 2026-11-15. The chart itself is generated to `world/type-chart.md` when the quest is locked; this file holds the why behind each matchup and is not overwritten.

## The two ideas

- **Mains are materials.** A main beats another main only if something physical happens when they meet.
- **Secondaries are lifestyles.** A secondary beats something only if that way of living would win in the wild.

## The other rules

- Every 2× has a ½× going back the other way.
- Mains absorb their own element: a Fire move on a Fire mon heals it a little instead of doing damage.
- Two mutual matchups: Light and Dark hit each other hard, and so do Bug and Hearth (pests against pest control).
- Day and night flip Light and Dark: by day only Light's 2× applies, by night only Dark's. The chart shows both; the battle system applies the flip.
- Seven immunities: Lightning can't hit Ground (earthed) or Crystal (insulator), Ground can't hit Flying (can't reach), Poison can't hit Spirit (can't poison the dead), Spore can't hit Metal (nothing to rot) or Ice (nothing rots when frozen), Star can't hit Air (burns up before it lands).

## Weakest reasoning (revisit first)

- Relic's three hits share a single idea ("it survived worse") and were partly chosen because Poison, Fire and Air had room for another weakness.
- Lightning beats Air and Water beats Star are thin; both exist mainly so Air and Star have enough weaknesses.
- Lightning and Star have only 2 weaknesses each, the fewest on the chart. Their extra immunities (Crystal, Air) are there to offset that.
- Crystal and Metal rest on one distinction: crystal is hard, so it cuts metal, but brittle, so Fighting breaks it.
