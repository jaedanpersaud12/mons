## How types work

- **Main types** are real-world elements that pool at a source. Every mon has exactly one: the element it absorbs.
- **Secondary types** come from where and how a mon lives inside its biome: depth, altitude, night, caves, behaviour. They are optional.
- A mon that lives where two elements meet (a volcano, where fire meets stone) can take a second main type in the secondary slot instead.
- Examples: a deep-water mon is **Water / Dark**. A flyer from the storm peaks is **Lightning / Flying**.
